## Purpose

These personas translate the LoanEase research findings into representative user archetypes that can guide subsequent UX decisions.

They are based on patterns identified through the survey and semi-structured interviews.

They are not intended to represent real individual participants.

The personas focus on differences in user needs, behaviours, goals, and pain points rather than relying only on demographic differences.

---

# Persona 1 — The Clarity-Seeking Applicant

## Profile

**Archetype:** First-time or less experienced digital loan applicant

**Primary need:** Understand eligibility, financial implications, and next steps before committing.

**Experience level:** Limited or moderate previous loan experience.

---

## Context

This user approaches the loan process with uncertainty.

They may not fully understand:

* Eligibility requirements.
* Financial terminology.
* Interest rates.
* Loan terms.
* Required documentation.
* What happens after submitting an application.

They are willing to consider a digital loan platform but need clear guidance before making important financial decisions.

---

## Goals

* Understand whether they are eligible.
* Understand the financial commitment.
* Know what documents are required.
* Complete the application correctly.
* Avoid unexpected fees or requirements.
* Understand what happens at each stage.
* Feel confident before submitting sensitive information.

---

## Pain Points

* Eligibility criteria can be difficult to understand.
* Financial terminology can be confusing.
* Fees and charges may not be sufficiently clear.
* Document requirements can create uncertainty.
* Lack of guidance can make the application feel complicated.
* Uncertainty can reduce confidence.

---

## Needs

* Clear eligibility guidance.
* Plain-language explanations.
* Transparent fee and interest information.
* EMI/repayment calculation.
* Clear document requirements.
* Step-by-step guidance.
* Reassurance before important actions.

---

## Motivations

* Make an informed financial decision.
* Avoid mistakes.
* Understand affordability.
* Complete the process efficiently.
* Feel confident that the application is being handled correctly.

---

## Behaviours

Research suggests this user may seek additional information before committing to a financial decision.

Potential behaviours to account for include:

* Checking eligibility before completing the full application.
* Looking for explanations of unfamiliar terms.
* Comparing financial implications.
* Seeking reassurance before submitting documents.

These behaviours should be validated during usability testing rather than treated as universal behaviour.

---

## Representative Quote

> "I want to understand everything clearly before I submit my application."

This is a representative synthesis statement, not a direct participant quotation.

---

## Design Implications

The experience should explore:

* Eligibility checker.
* Clear explanations of financial terms.
* EMI calculator.
* Transparent fees and charges.
* Document checklist.
* Step-by-step guidance.
* Contextual help.

# Persona 2 — The Progress-Focused Applicant

## Profile

**Archetype:** Applicant with previous loan or application experience

**Primary need:** Know what is happening after submitting an application.

**Experience level:** Moderate or high previous loan experience.

---

## Context

This user is more familiar with the general loan process but becomes frustrated when the digital experience provides insufficient visibility after application submission.

The major issue is not necessarily understanding how loans work.

It is knowing:

* Whether the application was received.
* What stage it is currently in.
* Whether additional information is required.
* How long approval may take.
* When the next update will arrive.

---

## Goals

* Submit the application without unnecessary repetition.
* Know that documents were received.
* Track application progress.
* Understand expected approval timelines.
* Receive timely updates.
* Resolve issues quickly.
* Avoid repeatedly contacting support for status information.

---

## Pain Points

* Long approval waiting periods.
* Lack of application status updates.
* Repeated document submission.
* Unclear approval timelines.
* Inconsistent communication.
* Difficulty obtaining reliable status information.
* Dependence on customer support for updates.

---

## Needs

* Real-time application status.
* Clear progress stages.
* Estimated approval timeline.
* Document submission status.
* Notifications.
* Clear next actions.
* Reliable support.

---

## Motivations

* Maintain control over the application.
* Know what is happening without repeatedly asking.
* Avoid unnecessary delays.
* Complete the process efficiently.
* Receive reliable information.

---

## Behaviours

Research suggests this user is particularly sensitive to delays and lack of communication after submission.

Potential behaviours to account for include:

* Checking application status.
* Looking for progress updates.
* Contacting support when information is unavailable.
* Monitoring whether additional documents are required.

These behaviours should be validated during usability testing rather than treated as universal behaviour.

---

## Representative Quote

> "I've submitted everything. I just want to know what is happening with my application."

This is a representative synthesis statement, not a direct participant quotation.

---

## Design Implications

The experience should explore:

* Application status tracker.
* Progress indicators.
* Estimated approval timeline.
* Document status.
* Automated notifications.
* Clear next-step instructions.
* Accessible customer support.

# Persona Comparison

| Dimension             | Clarity-Seeking Applicant       | Progress-Focused Applicant      |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Primary concern       | Understanding before committing | Knowing what is happening       |
| Main stage            | Eligibility → Application       | Submission → Approval           |
| Major pain point      | Confusion and uncertainty       | Waiting and lack of visibility  |
| Key need              | Clarity                         | Control                         |
| Financial information | Needs explanation               | Needs accessible reference      |
| Documentation         | Needs to know what is required  | Needs to know what was received |
| Status                | Wants expectations              | Wants real-time visibility      |
| Support               | Guidance                        | Resolution / updates            |
| Key opportunity       | Simplify decision-making        | Improve progress transparency   |

---

# Shared Needs

Although the personas have different primary concerns, both need:

* Clear information.
* Transparency.
* Reliable communication.
* Simple language.
* Confidence.
* Easy access to support.
* Trust when handling sensitive information.

Therefore, the personas should not lead to two completely separate products.

Instead, they highlight different priorities within the same LoanEase experience.

---

# Why These Personas Are Useful

The personas represent two different moments of uncertainty:

### Persona 1

**"Can I and should I apply?"**

### Persona 2

**"What is happening with my application?"**

This distinction creates a useful design framework:

```text
BEFORE / DURING APPLICATION
        ↓
Clarity
Eligibility
Financial understanding
Documents
        ↓
AFTER SUBMISSION
        ↓
Control
Status
Timeline
Communication
Next actions
```

# Evidence Basis

The personas are informed by:

* Survey responses.
* Survey analysis.
* Interview analysis.
* Final research report.
* Research synthesis.

Important supporting findings include:

* 9 participants reported difficulty understanding eligibility criteria.
* 10 reported a lengthy approval process.
* 7 reported lack of application status updates.
* 8 identified waiting for approval as the most difficult stage.
* 27 requested real-time application status tracking.
* 26 requested an EMI calculator.
* 25 requested an eligibility checker.
* 22 requested document upload progress.
* 21 requested an estimated approval timeline.
* Interviews reinforced themes around eligibility, documentation, approval delays, transparency, and communication.

---

# Limitations

These personas are research-informed archetypes rather than representations of individual research participants.

The available research does not establish that all first-time applicants share the characteristics of Persona 1 or that all experienced applicants share the characteristics of Persona 2.

The personas should therefore be treated as design tools and validated through subsequent usability testing and design evaluation.

---

# Next Step

Use these personas to identify:

1. The most important user journey.
2. The key task to map.
3. Major pain points at each journey stage.
4. Opportunities where the two personas have different needs.

The next UX artifact will be the **User Journey / Key Flow**, with particular attention to the transition from eligibility through application, document submission, waiting, and approval.
