## Purpose

This document records the purpose, scope, and outcome of the LoanEase survey research.

The document is reconstructed from the completed survey analysis and research report.

---

## Survey Objective

The survey was used to identify broad patterns in users' experiences and expectations around loan applications.

The survey aimed to understand:

- Previous loan experience.
- Application channels.
- Satisfaction.
- Major pain points.
- Difficult application stages.
- Desired digital features.
- Importance of real-time updates.
- Confidence when uploading documents.
- Future adoption intent.

---

## Participant Responses

- Total responses received: 42
- Valid responses analysed: 41
- Excluded response: 1 due to lack of participant consent

---

## Participant Profile

The analysed participants included:

- 18–25: 22
- 26–35: 16
- 36–45: 3

Occupation distribution included:

- Salaried employees: 28
- Students: 9
- Self-employed: 3
- Homemaker/household manager: 1

Previous loan experience:

- Yes: 19
- No: 22

---

## Key Survey Findings

### Major Challenges

| Challenge | Responses |
|---|---:|
| Lengthy approval process | 10 |
| Understanding eligibility criteria | 9 |
| Lack of application status updates | 7 |
| Collecting required documents | 6 |
| Poor customer support | 5 |
| Technical issues while applying | 4 |
| Hidden charges or unclear fees | 4 |
| Difficulty understanding loan terms | 4 |

### Most Difficult Stage

| Stage | Responses |
|---|---:|
| Waiting for approval | 8 |
| Checking eligibility | 4 |
| Uploading documents | 3 |
| None of the above | 2 |
| Filling the application | 1 |
| Understanding the final decision | 1 |

---

## Desired Features

| Feature | Responses |
|---|---:|
| Real-time application status tracking | 27 |
| EMI calculator | 26 |
| Loan eligibility checker | 25 |
| Live chat / customer support | 24 |
| Document upload progress | 22 |
| Estimated approval timeline | 21 |
| Simple explanations of loan terms | 19 |
| Loan comparison tool | 16 |

---

## Real-Time Updates

Responses to the importance of real-time updates were:

- Extremely Important: 26
- Important: 8
- Neutral: 1
- Slightly Important: 2
- Not Important: 4

This indicates that status communication is a significant user expectation.

---

## Confidence in Document Upload

Responses included:

- Very Confident: 14
- Confident: 9
- Neutral: 6
- Slightly Confident: 5
- Not Confident: 7

This suggests that trust and confidence around sensitive document submission should be considered during design.

---

## Future Adoption

- Yes: 36
- Maybe: 5
- No: 0

This suggests strong willingness to use a well-designed digital loan platform among the surveyed participants.

---

## Survey Limitations

- 41 valid responses were analysed.
- The sample may not represent all loan applicants.
- Geographic coverage may be limited.
- Survey responses represent participant-reported experiences.
- Observation was not part of the completed research.

---

## Design Relevance

The survey provides evidence for exploring:

- Application status tracking.
- Eligibility guidance.
- EMI calculation.
- Document progress.
- Approval timelines.
- Customer support.
- Clear financial explanations.

These findings will be combined with interview insights before final design priorities are established.
