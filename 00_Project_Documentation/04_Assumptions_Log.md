## Purpose

This document records the assumptions that influenced the early direction of the LoanEase project and compares them with evidence gathered through primary research.

The purpose is to distinguish assumptions from validated findings and reduce hindsight bias.

## A-01 — Users Experience Friction

### Initial assumption

Users experience friction during digital loan applications.

### Evidence

The survey identified:

- Lengthy approval process — 10 responses.
- Difficulty understanding eligibility — 9.
- Lack of status updates — 7.
- Document collection — 6.

Interviews also identified approval delays, documentation challenges, communication problems, and transparency issues.

### Outcome

**Supported.**

### Design implication

The design should focus on reducing uncertainty and friction at key stages of the application journey.

---

## A-02 — Transparency Improves Confidence

### Initial assumption

Greater transparency would improve user confidence.

### Evidence

Participants repeatedly highlighted:

- Lack of status updates.
- Approval uncertainty.
- Unclear fees and charges.
- Inconsistent information.
- Lack of communication.

Real-time updates were rated highly important by most survey participants.

### Outcome

**Supported.**

### Design implication

Progress information, clear explanations, and transparent communication should be considered core parts of the experience.

---

## A-03 — Simplifying Financial Information

### Initial assumption

Simplifying financial information would improve usability.

### Evidence

Research identified difficulty understanding:

- Eligibility criteria.
- Interest rates.
- Loan terms.
- Charges and fees.

Participants also requested an eligibility checker, EMI calculator, and simple explanations.

### Outcome

**Supported as a design opportunity.**

The research supports the need for clearer financial information, although usability improvement must ultimately be validated through prototype testing.

### Design implication

Use plain language, contextual explanations, transparent calculations, and clear fee/interest breakdowns.

---

## A-04 — Research Will Guide Design

### Initial assumption

User research would provide sufficient evidence to guide design decisions.

### Evidence

The survey and interviews produced converging findings and concrete design opportunities.

Examples include:

- Status tracking.
- Eligibility guidance.
- Document progress.
- Approval timeline.
- EMI calculation.
- Support.

### Outcome

**Supported.**

### Design implication

Design decisions should be explicitly linked to research evidence wherever possible.

---

## Research Method Assumption

Observation was considered as a possible research method based on the original assignment context.

### Outcome

Observation was not conducted.

### Reason

The project involved financial applications and potentially private banking activity. Observing real users during such activities would raise privacy and ethical concerns.

### Alternative

The project used:

- Online survey research.
- Semi-structured interviews.
- Quantitative analysis.
- Qualitative thematic analysis.
