## Purpose

This document records the purpose and outcome of the semi-structured interview research conducted for LoanEase.

The document is reconstructed from the completed interview analysis and final research report.

---

## Interview Objective

The interviews were intended to provide deeper qualitative understanding of users' experiences with loan applications.

The interviews complemented the survey by exploring:

- Experiences behind reported problems.
- Approval delays.
- Communication issues.
- Documentation difficulties.
- Eligibility confusion.
- Interest rates and charges.
- Transparency.
- Trust and confidence.

---

## Method

Semi-structured interviews were used.

This approach allowed recurring topics to be explored while giving participants the opportunity to describe their experiences in their own words.

---

## Participants

A total of:

**12 participants**

took part in the interviews.

Participants represented different occupations and loan experiences, including personal, home, car, business, and education loans.

---

## Key Interview Themes

### Approval Delays and Lack of Updates

Participants frequently described long waiting periods and limited communication regarding application progress.

### Eligibility and Interest Rate Confusion

Several participants reported difficulty understanding eligibility criteria, CIBIL score impact, and interest-rate calculations.

### Documentation Challenges

Participants described repeated document submissions, changing requirements, and difficulty managing paperwork.

### Lack of Transparency

Participants wanted clearer explanations of loan terms, foreclosure charges, hidden fees, and approval criteria.

### Communication Issues

Inconsistent information across branches, online portals, and customer support created confusion.

### Special Circumstances

One participant described difficulties associated with overseas application and cross-country document handling.

---

## Design Opportunities Identified

| User Problem | Potential Design Opportunity |
|---|---|
| Long approval time with no updates | Real-time status tracker and notifications |
| Confusing eligibility criteria | Eligibility checker with simple explanations |
| Unclear interest rates and charges | Transparent loan summary and fee breakdown |
| Multiple document submissions | Document manager and upload progress |
| Inconsistent information | Centralised information hub |
| Poor communication | Live chat and in-app notifications |
| Difficulty understanding terminology | Plain-language explanations and contextual help |

---

## Relationship to Survey Research

The interviews broadly supported the survey findings.

The two methods converged around:

- Approval uncertainty.
- Status communication.
- Eligibility.
- Documentation.
- Transparency.
- Financial clarity.

This convergence strengthens confidence that these themes should be considered during research synthesis.

---

## Limitations

- Twelve participants were interviewed.
- Participants may not represent all loan applicants.
- Interviews depend on participant recollection and self-reporting.
- The study did not include observation of real financial transactions.
