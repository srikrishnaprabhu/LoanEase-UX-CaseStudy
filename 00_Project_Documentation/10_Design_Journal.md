## Purpose

This document will record design decisions, iterations, alternatives considered, feedback received, and lessons learned during the UX/UI design phase of LoanEase.

The journal is intended to preserve the reasoning behind the final design rather than only documenting the final screens.

---

## Current Status

The project has completed the initial research phase and is currently undergoing retrospective and documentation review.

Detailed design work has not yet been documented in this journal.

No historical design decisions are being fabricated.

---

## Design Documentation Format

Each significant design decision should be recorded using the following structure:

### Design Decision

What was changed or decided?

### Problem Addressed

What user problem or research finding motivated the decision?

### Evidence

What research, usability feedback, or project evidence supports it?

### Alternatives Considered

What other approaches were considered?

### Decision

Why was the selected approach chosen?

### Result

What happened after implementation or testing?

### Learning

What was learned from the decision?

## Planned Design Areas

The research currently suggests several areas for exploration:

- Application status tracking.
- Eligibility guidance.
- EMI calculation.
- Document upload progress.
- Approval timeline.
- Financial terminology explanations.
- Fee and interest transparency.
- Customer support.
- Trust and security communication.

These are design opportunities, not final design decisions.

They will be prioritised after research synthesis.
